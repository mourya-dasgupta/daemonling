# Daemonling for VS Code

A tiny daemon that lives on your focus. Your pet sits in the status bar, shows its mood and the time
left in your focus session, and gently notices when you wander off. It shares its home
(`~/.daemonling`) with the `dmn` command-line tool.

See the [user guide](https://github.com/mourya-dasgupta/daemonling/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md) and the
[Privacy Promise](https://github.com/mourya-dasgupta/daemonling/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
